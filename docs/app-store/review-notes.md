# Notes for App Review

**English** (App Review reads English):

- Contrarreloj is a Spanish timed quiz game. No account is needed to play: tap "Jugar arcade".
- Competitive mode and the ranking need Sign in with Apple or Google ("Modo competitivo" or
  "Entrar"). Any Apple ID works; there is no separate demo account.
- Account deletion: Ajustes (Settings tab) › Cuenta › Eliminar cuenta. It asks to sign in again,
  then deletes the player's ranking entries and the account.
- User-generated content: the ranking shows the names and photos of other players. Each row has a
  menu with "Reportar jugador" (opens an email to us) and "Ocultar jugador" (hides them on the
  device; hidden players can be shown again in Ajustes). Contact: informacion@contrarreloj-quiz.com.
- Analytics (Firebase/Google Analytics) is off until the player accepts it; no IDFA and no tracking,
  so there is no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
- No in-app purchases and no ads.

**Español**: arcade sin cuenta; competitivo con Apple o Google; borrado en Ajustes › Cuenta ›
Eliminar cuenta; reportar y ocultar jugadores en el ranking; analítica solo con consentimiento.
